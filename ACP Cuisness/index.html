Indian Food

Indian cuisine is known for its rich flavors, spices and variety. Different regions of India have their own unique and delicious cuisines.

  • Bengali
  • Gujarati
  • Mughlai
  • Punjabi
  • South Indian

Bengali Food

Bengali cuisine is appreciated for its fabulous use of pungent spices, aromatic rice and a wide variety of fish. Bengali food includes rice, fish, vegetables and delicious sweets.

Gujarati Food

The traditional Gujarati food is primarily vegetarian and has a high nutritional value. The typical Gujarati meal consists of many delicious dishes.

Mughlai Food

Mughlai cuisine is one of the most popular cuisines, whose origin can be traced back to the times of Mughal Empire. Mughlai dishes are rich and flavorful.

Punjabi Food

The cuisine of Punjab has an enormous variety of mouth-watering vegetarian as well as non-vegetarian dishes. The people enjoy dishes such as naan, paneer and butter chicken.

South Indian Cuisine

The cuisine of South India is known for its light, low calorie preparations. The traditional food is cooked in a unique way. Dosa, idli and sambar are popular South Indian dishes.
