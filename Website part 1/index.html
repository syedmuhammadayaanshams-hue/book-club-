Chineese Cuisine

Chineese cuisine is an important part of Chineese culture, which includes cuisine originating from the diverse regions of china, as well as from chineese people in oter parts of the world. Because of the chineese diaspora and historical power of the country chinese cuisine has influenced many other cuisines in Asia, with modifications made to cater to loal palates. chinese food staples such as rice, soy sauce , noodles, tea and tofu, and utensils such as chopsticks and the wok, can now be found worldwide
