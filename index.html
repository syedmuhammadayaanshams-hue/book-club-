Indian Food

Indian cuisine is known for its rich flavors, spices and variety. Different regions of India have their own unique and delicious cuisines.

Bengali Food

Bengali cuisine is famous for its fish dishes, rice, sweets and flavorful spices.

Gujarati Food

Gujarati cuisine is known for dishes such as dhokla, thepla and a variety of sweet and savory foods.

Mughlai Food

Mughlai cuisine is rich and flavorful, with dishes such as biryani, kebabs and creamy curries.

Punjabi Food

Punjabi cuisine is popular for foods such as naan, butter chicken, chole and other hearty dishes.

South Indian Cuisine

South Indian cuisine includes delicious dishes such as dosa, idli, vada and sambar.
