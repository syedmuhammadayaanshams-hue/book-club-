Indian Cuisines

The cuisine of India is diverse and varies according to different regions and cultures. Indian food is famous for its spices, flavors and variety.

  • Bengali
  • Gujarati
  • Mughlai
  • Punjabi
  • South Indian

Bengali Food

Bengali cuisine is appreciated for its fabulous use of pungent spices, aromatic rice and a wide variety of fish. Bengali food is rich in flavor.

Gujarati Food

The traditional Gujarati food is primarily vegetarian and has a high nutritional value. Gujarati cuisine includes many delicious sweet and savory dishes.

Mughlai Food

Mughlai cuisine is one of the most popular cuisines, whose origin can be traced back to the times of the Mughal Empire. Mughlai dishes are rich and flavorful.

Punjabi Food

The cuisine of Punjab has an enormous variety of mouth-watering vegetarian as well as non-vegetarian dishes. Punjabi food is full of flavor.

South Indian Cuisine

The cuisine of South India is known for its light, low-calorie preparations. Dosa, idli, vada and sambar are popular South Indian dishes.
